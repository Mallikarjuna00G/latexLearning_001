	Solve the following recurrence for $n, k\geq 0$.
	
	% starred variant (with a *) disables equation numbering.
	\begin{align*}
		Q_{n, 0} &= 1 \quad Q_{0, k} = [k = 0]; \\
		Q_{n, k} &= Q_{n-1, k} + Q_{n-1, k-1} + \binom{n}{k}, \quad\text{for $n$, $k>0$.}
	\end{align*}
