\documentclass{article}
\usepackage[T1]{fontenc}
\usepackage{babel}

\begin{document}
	
	\section{Testing German}
	
	German is great for testing \textbf{hyphenation} and the \textbf{eszett (ß)}.
	
	\selectlanguage{german}
	Alles hat ein Ende, nur die Wurst hat zwei. Wer rastet, der rostet, doch Übung macht den Meister.
	
	\selectlanguage{english}
	\textbf{Translation:} Everything has an end, only the sausage has two. He who rests, rusts, but practice makes the master.
	
	\section{Testing French}
	
	French is excellent for testing \textbf{ligatures} and \textbf{special spacing} (French typography requires a space before colons and exclamation marks).
	
	\selectlanguage{french}
	Le plus grand faible des hommes, c'est l'amour qu'ils ont pour la vie. Il faut manger pour vivre, et non pas vivre pour manger.
	
	\selectlanguage{english}
	\textbf{Translation:} The greatest weakness of men is the love they have for life. One must eat to live, and not live to eat.
